# Oliveira et al. 2020 source audit

## Decision

Register as unresolved candidate `C020`; do not count it in the direct benchmark. The paper reports 18 networks—galling, sucking, and chewing assemblages for each of six Neotropical savanna areas—but open, site-resolved interaction data and sufficient guild-specific methods have not yet been reconstructed.

## Source identity

- Article: Oliveira et al., “Comparing the plant–herbivore network topology of different insect guilds in Neotropical savannas,” *Ecological Entomology* (2020), DOI `10.1111/een.12808`.
- Reported corpus: six areas × three herbivore guilds = 18 networks, 317 insect morphospecies, 50 plant species, and 489 distinct interactions.
- Advertised supplement: `een12808-sup-0001-AppendixS1.docx`, described by the journal as a checklist of host-plant and insect species.

## Evidence-layer issue

The galling networks may qualify as E0 if plant-specific diagnostic galls were recorded within each bounded site. Sucking and chewing insects require separate methods review: collection from vegetation cannot be upgraded to direct feeding without observation, damage, rearing, or another permitted diagnostic. The three guilds must therefore never be assigned one evidence class by convenience.

## Data-access issue

The journal exposes the supplement metadata, but direct attempts to retrieve the DOCX returned access-denied responses. No independent open repository containing a site-level edge table has yet been identified. Even if the checklist becomes downloadable, a global checklist would not be sufficient to reconstruct the six site-specific networks.

## Required next action

Recover an openly accessible article or author manuscript with full methods and a site-resolved plant–insect table. Screen the six gall networks as prospective E0 objects and the twelve sucking/chewing networks independently. If only an aggregate checklist is available, retain the source as enrichment (`X`) rather than constructing local networks by inference.
